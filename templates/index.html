Intelligent Learning Workflow

How the AI recommendation cycle personalizes your study journey

1. Academic Scoping

Select Branch, Year, and Semester. Only subjects and topics belonging to your current syllabus are loaded, avoiding curriculum confusion.

2. Diagnostic Assessment

Take a comprehensive 54-question diagnostic quiz covering Easy, Medium, and Hard questions linked to every unit and topic in Semester V.

3. AI Recommendation

Pandas calculates topic accuracy (<40% Weak, 40-70% Average, >70% Strong) while Scikit-Learn Decision Trees predict learning pathways.
